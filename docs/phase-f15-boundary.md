# Phase F15 boundary

## Included

- Partial or full credit-note drafts against the remaining unpaid balance of an issued invoice
- Ray-only, context-bound confirmation before credit-note issuance
- Deterministic A4 credit-note DOCX/PDF output derived from the approved currency invoice template
- Explicit, context-bound cancellation of unpaid issued invoices and issued quotations
- Immutable cancellation records, replacement links, status history, issuance attempts, and number allocations
- Replacement invoice and quotation drafts that visibly reference the cancelled original
- Credit-aware summaries, invoice registers, outstanding reports, and overdue reports
- Private Drive filing for issued credit notes and immutable cancellation/replacement manifests

## Explicitly not included

- Deleting, overwriting, moving, or renumbering any original issued document
- Refunds, cash disbursements, payment reversal, write-offs, or bank actions
- Cancelling paid or partially paid invoices
- Automatic customer delivery or WhatsApp mutation tools
- Currency conversion, tax changes, reconciliation, or F16 production-hardening work
- Changes to Jessie, other agents, routing, OAuth, Gmail, Calendar, Notion, or coaching memory
