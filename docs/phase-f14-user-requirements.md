# Recorded requirements for Phase F14

These requirements were supplied during F12/F13 and are recorded only. Phase F13 does not implement them.

- A claim may be marked for client/company recharge and allocated to a specific company and project.
- Recharge inclusion in an outgoing invoice must require Ray's explicit confirmation.
- The link from an approved claim to the resulting outgoing invoice line must be immutable and auditable.
- Mira should prepare a monthly company claim-submission pack containing a summary and the supporting receipts.
- F14 design must define pending, approved, invoiced, excluded, and submitted states without changing historical issued invoices.
